# Screws and fixings

Self-tapping into printed pilot holes; no heat-set inserts or nuts are required
for the documented shell. Pilots are 2.6mm for M3 and 1.6mm for M2.
Use suitable pan-head screws and check engagement before tightening.

| Screw | Quantity | Location |
|---|---|---|
| M3×10 | 4 | Lid to corner posts |
| M3×8 | 2 | USB-C breakout to side-wall ledge |
| M2×6 | 4 | Selected V1 or V2 carrier to floor posts |
| M2×6 | 2 | Speaker tabs to front bosses |
| M2×6 | 1, or 2 with optional upper screw | RC522 pin end to card-guide bosses |
| M2×6 | 4, V1 only | External SD module to left-wall posts |

Totals without the optional upper RFID screw: **M3×10 ×4, M3×8 ×2**, and
**M2×6 ×11 for V1** or **×7 for V2**. Add one M2×6 if using that upper screw.
The [offset shim](rc522-8mm-offset-shim.md) uses two M2×6 screws in total for
its reader mounting sequence, replacing the direct reader-to-shell fixing.

Only the chosen carrier pattern prints in the matching shell STL. Individual
CPU boards attach to the carrier assembly; the floor screws locate that assembly.
